Houston, TX – AMSYS Innovative Solutions is proud to announce that its Founder, Chairman, and CEO, Khalid Parekh (KP), has been named a Founding Member of the NVIDIA Global Enterprise AI Partner Advisory Board. This distinguished appointment places Mr Parekh among a select group of global executives who will collaborate with NVIDIA to help shape the strategic direction of enterprise AI.

Headquartered in Houston, Texas, AMSYS Innovative Solutions is a global technology solutions provider specializing in Managed IT Services, Cybersecurity, AI, Cloud, AV, and Smart Infrastructure. With over 22 years of innovation and a 97% customer retention rate, AMSYS serves Fortune 500 companies, government entities, and enterprises worldwide. Post this collaboration, AMSYS is poised to further advance cutting-edge AI solutions and accelerate digital transformation for its clients.

In Khalid Parekh’s words, “Joining the NVIDIA Partner Advisory Board is both an honor and a responsibility to help accelerate innovation and unlock the transformative potential of AI for enterprises worldwide, and at AMSYS, we remain deeply committed to driving digital transformation for our clients, and this collaboration will further enhance our ability to contribute to the global AI ecosystem.” In this role, he will provide guidance on partner strategy, innovation initiatives, and large-scale enterprise adoption of advanced AI technologies.

Khalid “KP” Parekh — Entrepreneur, technology visionary, and Founder & CEO of AMSYS Group.
