## Rules

- Use only free profiles the business can claim, or coverage and invitations that are earned.
- Membership dues, sponsorships, and “contributor” packages that are sold as a link are out of scope. If a membership already exists, updating that member profile is in scope. Buying one for a link is not.
- One real listing per business and per location. Do not create a second Google, Bing, or Apple listing for an office that is not confirmed.
- Use the brand names in ordinary sentences. Do not repeat them as anchor text, and do not buy or swap links.
- Profiles that already exist are updated in place. They are not recreated.
- Every draft in this file is marked **DRAFT — needs approval.**
