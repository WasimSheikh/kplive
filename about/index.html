Origin

From $100 to $350 Million

Khalid Parekh’s entrepreneurial journey began with a single principle: our word is our honor.

In the early 2000s, Khalid started AMSYS with no outside funding, no investors, and no safety net. What he had was a commitment to delivering exactly what he promised — every time, to every client. That principle became the foundation everything else was built on.

Over the next two decades, AMSYS grew from a one-person IT services firm into a multi-vertical enterprise technology group spanning managed IT services, cybersecurity, healthcare technology, insurance technology, hospitality solutions, and energy sector infrastructure. Today AMSYS employs hundreds of professionals and maintains a 97% client retention rate — a number that reflects the trust built over 22 years of consistent delivery.

Values

Philosophy and Values

Khalid Parekh believes that the businesses which last are the ones communities trust.

His approach to leadership is built on three principles. First, deliver exactly what you promise — no gap between commitment and execution. Second, serve the community, not just the client — every product and service should genuinely improve the lives of the people it is designed for. Third, lead with data — every decision at AMSYS is backed by evidence, not assumption.

Khalid Parekh on AI and the Future of Technology

As a member of NVIDIA’s Global Enterprise AI Partner Advisory Board, Khalid Parekh sits at the intersection of frontier AI research and real-world enterprise implementation. He works with organizations across healthcare, finance, energy, and insurance to build AI strategies that deliver measurable ROI — not just proof-of-concept pilots.

His perspective on AI is practical: the technology is only as valuable as the governance around it. Transparency, human oversight, and ongoing accountability are not constraints on innovation. They are the conditions for sustainable innovation.
